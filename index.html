India · 2021–2026

Thirteen movements. Six years. Every one of them applied pressure to the same political system, and the system answered them very differently. This terminal measures how hard each one pushed, how hard the state pushed back, and which institution, if any, finally moved.

Read this first. These are thirteen purposively selected high-salience cases, not a sample. ACLED logged roughly 20,600 demonstrations in India in 2025 alone. Nothing here estimates a national rate. The intensity index is constructed by this dashboard and its weights are yours to change.

Standing finding Pressure does not predict concession in this set. The 2024–25 farmers' agitation scored 73 and ran 400 days for nothing; the Noida wage strike scored 38 and ran nine days for a 20% statutory wage revision. What separates them is the price of the demand. A wage notification is an executive signature. A legal MSP guarantee is a permanent fiscal commitment that crosses a veto point no crowd can reach.
